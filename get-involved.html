What to expect

  1. 1 Sign up

    Pick a date on POINT. You will get the meeting place and a reminder.

  2. 2 Get ready

    Sign a short waiver. Wear closed shoes, long pants and a hat. Bring water.

  3. 3 Show up

    We hand you gloves, a vest and a job. Crew volunteers are 16 and up.

Bring your group

Churches, businesses, school clubs and families serve together all the time. Tell us your group size and we will find a date.

Internships

Students can earn experience, and often course credit, doing work that matters in their own city.

  • Nonprofit operations and volunteer management
  • Marketing, photo and video
  • Social work and community outreach
  • Grants and fundraising

Internships run by semester, about 8 hours a week. Openings are posted on Handshake.
