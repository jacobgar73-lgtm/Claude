Is this real, and where does the money go?

Love Thy Neighbor Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Harlingen, Texas. EIN {{ein}}. We are run by volunteers.

Gifts go to tools, supplies, fuel, disposal and insurance for the work. Volunteers have given time worth $15,370 so far. That is the value of their hours, not money raised.

Want the numbers behind the work? Contact us and we will walk you through them.
