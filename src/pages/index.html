Why this work is needed

Harlingen is in Cameron County, where many older adults live on very little and keep up a home on their own.

Census figures, 2020 to 2024

23%

Nearly one in four Cameron County residents 65 and older lives below the federal poverty level. That is about double the rate for Texas and the nation.

People 65 and older living below the poverty level

Cameron County 23%
Texas 12%
United States 11%

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020 to 2024, table S1701.

48%

Nearly half have incomes below twice the poverty level, compared with 28% nationwide.

Cameron County 48%
United States 28%

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table B17024.

1 in 4

About 14,500 residents 65 and older live alone. About two thirds of them are women.

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table B09020.

8 in 10

About 79% of households headed by someone 65 or older own their home. The upkeep falls on them.

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table S0103.

15 a day

The U.S. Surgeon General says lacking social connection affects the risk of early death about as much as smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day.

Source: Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation, U.S. Surgeon General's advisory, 2023. A national finding.
