Sign up on POINT

Sign-up runs on POINT, a volunteer app. Meeting places are shared after you sign up, to protect our neighbors' privacy.

Four ways to serve

Clean-up crews

Yard work, junk removal and outdoor maintenance on Saturday mornings. Ages 16 and up.

Adopt a neighbor

Keep in touch with one senior every month: letters, calls, coffee and care packages.

Hearts & Hands gatherings

Lead crafts, music and conversation at care facilities once a month. Ages 13 and up.

Good Works booth

Sort donated goods and help run our resale booth at local markets. Every purchase funds our programs.

Bring your group

Churches, businesses, school clubs and families are welcome to serve together. Tell us the size of your group and we will find a date.

What to expect

  1. Sign up

    Pick a date on POINT, or send the form below.

  2. Get ready

    Sign a liability waiver and join a short orientation.

  3. Show up

    We bring the tools. Most projects last a few hours.
