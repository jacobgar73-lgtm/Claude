The need in Cameron County

Harlingen is in Cameron County. Low incomes, hard-to-climb steps, a home to keep up and long days alone.

U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 to 2024

23%

Nearly one in four Cameron County residents 65 and older lives below the federal poverty level, about double the rate for Texas and the nation.

People 65 and older living below the poverty level

Cameron County 23%
Texas 12%
United States 11%

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020 to 2024, table S1701.

60,900

About 60,900 people aged 65 and over live in Cameron County, roughly one in seven residents, including about 11,800 in Harlingen.

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table DP05.

48%

Nearly half have incomes below twice the federal poverty level, compared with 28% nationwide.

Cameron County 48%
United States 28%

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table B17024.

26.5%

About one in four has serious difficulty walking or climbing stairs, compared with 20.6% nationally.

Cameron County 26.5%
United States 20.6%

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table S1810.

1 in 4

About 14,500 residents 65 and older live alone, nearly one in four. About two thirds of them are women.

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table B09020.

8 in 10

About 79% of households headed by someone 65 or older own their home, roughly 30,000 households. The upkeep falls on them.

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table S0103.

7 in 10

About 7 in 10 Cameron County residents 65 and older speak Spanish at home.

Source: Census Bureau, ACS 2020 to 2024, table B16004.

What research says about being alone and aging at home

National findings

15 a day

The U.S. Surgeon General says lacking social connection affects the risk of early death about as much as smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day.

Source: Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation, U.S. Surgeon General's advisory, 2023.

50%

Social isolation has been associated with about a 50 percent higher risk of developing dementia.

Source: National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, 2020.

29%

Poor social relationships have been associated with a 29% higher risk of coronary heart disease and a 32% higher risk of stroke.

Source: National Academies, 2020.

1 in 4

About 1 in 4 Americans 65 and older reports falling each year. Falls are the leading cause of injury-related death for that age group.

Source: CDC, Older Adult Falls Data.

75%

Three in four Americans aged 50 and over say they would like to stay in their current home for as long as possible.

Source: AARP Home and Community Preferences Survey, 2024.
