Questions families ask

What does it cost?

Nothing. The work is done by volunteers and paid for by donors, partners and a city grant.

Who qualifies?

Low-income seniors who own and live in a home in Harlingen. If you are not sure, send the form and we will sort it out together.

What work do you do?

We clear overgrown yards, trim trees, haul away junk and remove hazards outside the home.

What can you not do?

We do not do roof, structural, interior or mold repairs yet. We are also not a regular mowing service. If we cannot help, we will try to point you to someone who can.

How long does it take?

We call within three business days. Crews go out on Saturdays, so most homes are scheduled within four to six weeks.

Can I refer someone without telling them?

Please talk with them first if you can. We never show up without the homeowner's permission.

Will photos of the home be shared?

Only with a signed release. We never show house numbers, street signs or last names.

Does the senior need to share your faith?

No. We help neighbors because they are neighbors.
