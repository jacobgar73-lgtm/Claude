Neighbors helping neighbors, since 2025

Love Thy Neighbor is a volunteer-run, faith-based nonprofit in Harlingen, Texas. We serve our elderly with kindness and compassion.

Join us Contact us

How it started

Jacob Garcia worked as a life insurance agent. Visiting seniors, he kept seeing the same things: isolation, hard living conditions, no way to get around.

Love Thy Neighbor began in August 2025 to do something about it.

Our mission

We bring together volunteers, local partners and community members to meet the real and present needs of our neighbors. Through hands-on service, fellowship and outreach, we work to restore dignity, strengthen relationships and inspire hope.

Our faith

We believe true change begins when people put faith into action. Every act of service reflects our commitment to love like Christ: humbly, selflessly, and with a heart for lasting transformation across the Rio Grande Valley.

Where we are today

Fifteen months in. What is done, what is running and what comes next.

  1. August 2025 Love Thy Neighbor begins Founded in Harlingen by volunteers.
  2. October 2025 Recognized as a nonprofit Gifts become tax-deductible.
  3. February 2026 Valentine's fellowship, and our first press Games, cards and carnations for seniors. Explore Harlingen Magazine tells our story.
  4. August 2026 Restore clean-ups under way The first phase of Restore, Renew & Rebuild is active in Harlingen.
  5. Now Clean-ups and markets through December Four clean-ups and two Good Works markets are on the calendar.
  6. Next Renew: small repairs Painting, porch repair, ramps and railings.
  7. Later Rebuild: major repairs Roofing, siding, HVAC and plumbing.
