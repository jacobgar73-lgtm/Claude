About

Neighbors helping neighbors, since 2025.

Love Thy Neighbor is a volunteer-run nonprofit in Harlingen, Texas. We serve low-income seniors so they can stay safe in the homes they love.

Contact us

How it started

Jacob Garcia sold life insurance. The job took him into the homes of seniors across the Valley, and he kept seeing the same things. Yards that had gotten away from them. Quiet houses. No one to call.

In August 2025 he and a few friends stopped talking about it and started showing up. That was the first crew.

We don't want to just be a resource center. We don't want to just be a phone number. We want to be the help.

Jacob Garcia, president and co-founder, in Explore Harlingen Magazine, February 2026

Our mission

Building pathways of care and community wherever love is needed.

Our faith

We serve because we believe love is something you do. You do not need to share our faith to get help or to volunteer.
