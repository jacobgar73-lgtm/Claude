Refer a neighbor

Help us find the neighbors who need us most.

Do you know an elderly neighbor, friend or family member who could use a helping hand? Tell us about them. We handle every referral with care, confidentiality and respect.

Start a referral
What happens next

Simple, personal, confidential

  1. Share the detailsThe senior's name, location and a short description of their need.
  2. We review with careOur outreach team reviews every request.
  3. We reach outWe connect with the senior (or their family) to see how we can best help.

Help may include: home restoration or safety improvements, Adopt-A-Neighbor wellness visits and check-ins, and Hearts & Hands fellowship and prayer support.

Restore, Renew & Rebuild (Phase 1) is currently available in Harlingen, TX only.
